Outlook Web Bookmarklet

One click to import any email as a task — directly from Outlook Web.

Step 1 — Enter your details

Leave blank to use ntfy.sh push only (no tab switch)

Step 2 — Add to your bookmarks bar

Drag this button to your browser bookmarks bar:

📋 Add to Tasks

Can't drag? Do this instead:

  1. 1. Right-click the button → Copy Link Address
  2. 2. Open your bookmarks → Add new bookmark
  3. 3. Paste the link as the URL
  4. 4. Name it Add to Tasks

Step 3 — How to use it

1 Open outlook.office.com in your browser and click on any email
2 Click the 📋 Add to Tasks bookmark
3 A popup asks for the urgency level — pick one
4 The task appears in your tracker within seconds ✓

💬 Works on Teams Web too

The bookmarklet also works on teams.microsoft.com. It reads the page title and sends the task as a Teams source.
